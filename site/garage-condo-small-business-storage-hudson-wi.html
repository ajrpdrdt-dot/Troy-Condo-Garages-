Garage Condo for Small Business Storage in Hudson, WI: A Smarter Alternative to Commercial Leasing

If you run a contracting business, a landscaping operation, an HVAC company, an electrical service, or any other trade in Hudson, WI and the surrounding St. Croix County area, one of your biggest recurring costs is probably space. Equipment does not fit in a residential garage. A commercial lease in Hudson or the Twin Cities metro is expensive and locks you into someone else’s building. And outdoor storage is a liability in a Wisconsin climate.

A growing number of tradespeople and small business owners in western Wisconsin have found a better answer: a garage condo at Troy Condo Garages. This guide explains exactly why it makes sense — financially, operationally, and long-term.

The Commercial Lease Problem for Small Contractors in Hudson, WI

Commercial rental space along the I-94 corridor in St. Croix County does not come cheap. Industrial bay space near Hudson, WI typically runs anywhere from $800 to $2,500 per month depending on size, location, and lease terms. Add a triple-net lease structure and you are also paying property taxes, maintenance costs, and insurance contributions on top of base rent.

For a solo contractor or a small crew, this is an enormous overhead burden — especially in the early years of building a business. And unlike a mortgage, every rental payment goes to a landlord. You are building equity for someone else, not yourself.

A garage condo flips that dynamic. You finance the purchase, you own the unit, and your monthly payment is building equity in a real estate asset that you control.

Who Uses Garage Condos for Business Storage in Hudson, WI?

The range of trade and small business users at garage condo facilities like Troy Condo Garages is broader than most people expect. Common occupants include:

  • General contractors — Tool storage, lumber staging, small equipment like plate compactors and generators
  • Plumbers and HVAC technicians — Van and truck parking, pipe and fitting inventory, specialty tool storage
  • Electricians — Wire reels, panel inventory, conduit storage, service vehicle parking
  • Landscapers — Trailer storage, mower and equipment maintenance, off-season storage for seasonal machinery
  • Painters and finish contractors — Equipment, supply storage, and a clean workspace for prep work
  • Auto detailers — Equipment, chemical storage, and a professional-grade space to detail vehicles
  • Restoration and fabrication specialists — Project vehicles, welding setup, fabrication workspace

What all of these users share is the need for a secure, private, well-built space they can rely on — one that does not disappear when a landlord decides to sell the building or raise the rent.

What Business Owners Love About Troy Condo Garages

Height and Door Width for Service Vehicles

Many tradespeople drive full-size cargo vans, box trucks, or pickup trucks with racks and ladder systems that add significant roof height. Troy Condo Garages units are built with tall overhead doors and high ceilings that accommodate commercial service vehicles. You can pull your van in, close the door, and work in a private, weather-protected space — without the cramped, poorly lit conditions of a typical self-storage unit.

Your Own Electrical Service

Contractors use power tools constantly. Running a compressor, a welder, battery chargers for tool packs, and overhead lighting simultaneously requires real electrical capacity. Each unit at Troy Condo Garages has its own electrical panel, which means you are not fighting shared circuits or limited to what a standard storage facility allows.

Climate Control Options

Some tools and materials are sensitive to temperature extremes. Certain paints, adhesives, and coatings must be stored above freezing to remain usable. Pneumatic tools and air lines can crack in sustained cold. Owning a garage condo means you can install a heater for Wisconsin winters and maintain a workable environment year-round — something you simply cannot do in a standard rental unit.

Privacy and Security for Valuable Equipment

A commercial van full of tools represents tens of thousands of dollars in equipment. In a shared storage facility, you are one of many tenants with access to a common area. At Troy Condo Garages, your unit is private — access is controlled at the facility level, and your unit is your secured space. For contractors who carry expensive specialty tools or sensitive client materials, this level of security is a major factor.

Real-world scenario: A Hudson, WI plumber paying $1,200/month for a commercial bay has spent $72,000 over five years — with zero equity. A garage condo owner paying a comparable monthly mortgage payment has $72,000 of principal paid toward an asset they own. When the business grows and they need more space, they sell the unit (potentially at a profit) and move up. The renter just signs a new lease.

Zoning and Business Use Considerations

One of the most common questions from business owners considering a garage condo is whether business use is permitted. Garage condos like those at Troy Condo Garages are zoned for personal use storage and light activity — which means equipment storage, vehicle maintenance, and light workshop use are consistent with how these units are used across the industry.

If you have specific questions about permitted uses for your business type, the team at Troy Condo Garages can walk you through what is allowable. The key distinction: garage condos are not zoned for retail foot traffic, customer-facing operations, or manufacturing, but for contractors and tradespeople who need secure storage and a private workspace, they are an excellent fit.
