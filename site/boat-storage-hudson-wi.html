Boat Storage Near Hudson, WI: Why a Garage Condo Beats Outdoor Parking

Introduction

If you own a boat in western Wisconsin, you already know the drill. Every fall, you scramble for a storage spot. Every spring, you roll the dice hoping nothing was damaged over winter. Outdoor lots charge more each year, and the coverage they promise rarely matches the protection your investment deserves.

There is a better way. A growing number of boat owners near Hudson, WI are ditching seasonal storage rentals altogether and buying their own private garage condo at Troy Condo Garages. Instead of paying a landlord for access to a muddy lot, they own real estate — a deeded, climate-ready unit they control 365 days a year.

This guide breaks down exactly why private garage condo ownership has become the go-to boat storage solution in the St. Croix Valley, and what to look for when making the decision.

The Problem With Traditional Boat Storage in Wisconsin

Wisconsin winters are notoriously hard on boats. Freeze-thaw cycles crack fiberglass, moisture creeps into upholstery, and rodents find their way into engine compartments when a vessel sits exposed from November through April.

Most outdoor storage facilities in the Hudson and New Richmond area offer a tarp, a gravel pad, and a chain-link fence. That is it. Your boat sits in the elements while you pay monthly fees that compound year after year — with no equity, no control, and no guarantee your spot will be available next season.

Indoor options are limited. Most facilities that do offer covered storage have long waiting lists, and the few that accept boats large enough for the St. Croix River or Lake Pepin charge a significant premium with no ownership stake.

What Makes Troy Condo Garages Different for Boat Owners

Troy Condo Garages at 663 Valerie Ave, Hudson, WI was designed with oversized vehicles in mind. The facility features:

  • Oversized overhead doors (12′ x 14′) built for boats on trailers
  • Wide drive aisles with 60 feet of clearance — enough for even the largest lake boats
  • Pull-through unit options starting at 25′ x 100′ for easy maneuvering with a trailer
  • In-floor heating to protect hull, motor, and electronics over the winter
  • 150 amp electrical service in every unit — enough for battery chargers, dehumidifiers, and detail lighting
  • Water spigot and finished half bath for pre- and post-season cleanups
  • 24-hour video surveillance and gated entry

You are not renting a slot at a lot. You are buying a private, deeded space that functions as an extension of your home — one that appreciates in value alongside the real estate market.

Boat Storage Costs: Renting vs. Owning

Let’s look at the numbers honestly. Outdoor seasonal storage in the Hudson area typically runs $400–$800 per season for a standard boat slip or covered outdoor pad. Indoor storage, where available, can reach $1,200–$2,000+ annually. That adds up fast with nothing to show for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size units are available for boats?

Standard units run from 20’–25′ x 50′. Pull-through options start at 25′ x 100′. Multiple units can be combined for larger vessels. Troy’s team will help you select the right configuration for your specific boat and trailer setup.

Can I access my unit year-round?

Yes. Troy Condo Garages is a gated, secure facility with 24/7 access for unit owners. You are not dependent on seasonal operating hours or staff presence.

Is this a rental or ownership?

Ownership. Each unit is a deeded piece of real estate. You own it the way you own a home or commercial property, with all the financial and legal rights that come with that.

Are there HOA fees?

Yes. HOA dues cover facility maintenance, road upkeep, security, and insurance on your unit from the studs outward. The fee is structured to avoid nickel-and-diming owners on the amenities that matter most.
