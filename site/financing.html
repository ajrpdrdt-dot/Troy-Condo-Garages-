Financing options for your garage condo

Troy Condo Garages has partnered with a trusted local lender so you can finance your unit as real property.

Call Gordon Boos: 715-926-4234

A local lending partner who understands garage condos

Because Troy Condo Garages units are sold as real property — not rented as storage — buyers can finance their purchase much like other real estate investments.

We’ve connected with Alliance Bank in Mondovi, WI, a western Wisconsin community bank with a dedicated Senior Lending Officer ready to walk you through options.

How Alliance Bank can help

Real property purchase loans

Since garage condos are deeded real estate, they may qualify for financing similar to commercial or investment property.

Construction & build-out financing

Planning a custom mezzanine, lounge, or workshop? Ask Gordon about construction financing for interior improvements.

Business & line of credit

Using your unit for a hobby business or side venture? Alliance Bank also offers business lines of credit.

All loans subject to credit approval. Terms, rates, and eligibility are determined by Alliance Bank. Troy Condo Garages does not provide financing directly and receives no fee for this referral.
