Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

What is a garage condo?

A garage condo is a privately owned unit that provides secure storage and personal use space, such as a workshop or vehicle storage.

Are these units considered real property?

Yes, all units are garage condo units available for purchase — not rentals — and ownership is recorded as real property.

Can I store an RV or boat in a unit?

Absolutely. We offer oversized units designed for RVs, boats, and multiple vehicles.

Where are Troy Condo Garages located?

Our units are located at 663 Valerie Ave, Hudson, WI, convenient for Hudson residents and the Twin Cities area.
