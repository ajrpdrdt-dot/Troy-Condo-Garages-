Who Rentals Are For

A flexible, no-purchase-required option for anyone who needs secure, private garage space without committing to ownership.

Car Enthusiasts

High-clearance doors and room for a hydraulic lift, without buying a unit outright.

RV & Boat Owners

Oversized overhead doors sized for easy in-and-out maneuvering of larger vehicles.

Workshop & Hobby Use

Heavy-duty electrical for tools and machinery in a private, lockable space.

Overflow Storage

Seasonal gear, equipment, or business inventory that needs more room than a house garage.

What’s Included

Every rental unit comes with the same facility standards as our ownership units.

Security

24-hour video surveillance, gated entry, security fencing, and well-lit grounds.

Access

12′–14′ overhead garage doors and wide drive aisles — 60′ clearance fits even the largest RV.

Build Quality

Durable all-steel construction, concrete slab flooring, and an interior water spigot.
