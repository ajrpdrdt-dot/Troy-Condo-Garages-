Garage Condos Near Woodbury & Lake Elmo, MN: The Best Commute-Friendly Storage Option for Twin Cities Owners

If you live in Woodbury or Lake Elmo, MN and you have been searching for garage condos near Woodbury MN, chances are you have already discovered that the east metro has very few real options for owning — not renting — a private, climate-controlled space for your vehicles or collection. That is exactly why so many east metro buyers are making the short drive across the St. Croix River to Troy Condo Garages in Hudson, WI. In this guide, we will cover real drive times from Woodbury and Lake Elmo, what local buyers are typically storing, and why crossing state lines for ownership makes more financial sense than another year of local rental storage.

Why East Metro Owners Are Crossing the St. Croix

Woodbury and Lake Elmo are two of the fastest-growing communities in the Twin Cities east metro, and both are known for large-lot homes, boat and RV ownership, and residents who take their hobbies seriously. The problem is supply: local self-storage facilities in the east metro are almost entirely rental-based, offer little to no climate control for a full vehicle bay, and build zero equity no matter how many years you pay into them. Hudson, WI sits just minutes across the river, and it has quietly become the go-to destination for Twin Cities buyers who want ownership instead of another rent increase. We cover a similar trend from the northeast side of the metro in our article on garage condos near Stillwater, MN, and the pattern holds true for Woodbury and Lake Elmo as well.

Drive Time From Woodbury, MN to Hudson, WI

Woodbury sits just west of the St. Croix River, and thanks to I-94, the drive to Troy Condo Garages in Hudson is one of the most convenient cross-border commutes in the entire Twin Cities metro. Most Woodbury residents can expect to reach our Hudson, WI facility in roughly 15 to 20 minutes under normal traffic conditions, making it an easy weekend trip or even a quick after-work stop to check on a project vehicle or tow a trailer.

Drive Time From Lake Elmo, MN to Hudson, WI

Lake Elmo is positioned even closer to the state line than Woodbury, and residents typically reach Hudson, WI in about 15 minutes via I-94 East. For Lake Elmo's lake-and-land lifestyle, that short drive means boat owners, ATV enthusiasts, and car collectors can treat their garage condo almost like a second garage at home, without sacrificing the ownership upside of real property.

What Woodbury & Lake Elmo Owners Are Storing

Both communities sit close to the St. Croix River and a dense network of area lakes, so it is no surprise that boat and watercraft storage tops the list. But we see a wide mix of use cases from east metro buyers, including:

  • Boats pulled from Twin Cities-area lakes and the St. Croix for the off-season
  • RVs and campers that no longer fit in a Woodbury or Lake Elmo residential driveway or HOA
  • Classic and collector cars that need true climate control, not just a roof overhead
  • Woodworking, fabrication, and hobby workshops for owners who have outgrown a residential garage

Seasonal Storage Needs for East Metro Owners

Minnesota and Wisconsin winters put real pressure on anything left outside for months at a time, and Woodbury and Lake Elmo owners feel that pressure every year when boats, RVs, and bikes need to come off the driveway before the first snowfall. A garage condo solves this on a seasonal cycle rather than a one-time move:

  • Late fall: boats and pontoons come off area lakes and into climate-controlled storage before freeze-up
  • Winter: snowmobiles and ATVs rotate in as summer toys rotate out, all inside the same secured unit
  • Spring: RVs and campers get pulled out, serviced, and prepped for the first camping trip of the season
  • Summer: motorcycles, project cars, and workshop time take over the space during peak riding and wrenching season

Because the facility includes in-floor heating, energy-efficient lighting, and a full electrical panel, the same unit that stores a boat in November can double as a heated workshop in January. That kind of year-round flexibility is simply not available at a standard outdoor storage lot in the east metro, and it is a major reason Woodbury and Lake Elmo owners describe the short drive to Hudson as a worthwhile trade-off.

Why Wisconsin Real Estate Beats Local Rental Storage

The biggest difference between a Twin Cities self-storage rental and a Troy Condo Garages unit is not location — it is ownership structure. A rental facility in Woodbury or Lake Elmo is a recurring expense with no ceiling on future rate increases and nothing to show for it after ten years of payments. A garage condo purchase in Hudson, WI is titled real property, meaning every payment builds toward equity you can eventually sell or pass on. For a full breakdown of how that equity argument plays out over time, see our guide on whether a garage condo is a good investment, and our side-by-side garage condo versus storage unit comparison.

Buying in Wisconsin as a Minnesota Resident

A common question from Woodbury and Lake Elmo buyers is whether they can even purchase Wisconsin real estate as a Minnesota resident. The answer is yes — there is no residency requirement to purchase property in Wisconsin, and many of our current owners commute daily from the Twin Cities metro. Financing works similarly to any other out-of-state real estate purchase, though it is not identical to a standard home mortgage. Our guide on how to finance a garage condo in Hudson, WI walks through what Minnesota buyers should expect from lenders on this side of the river. Property taxes, HOA structure, and closing procedures also follow Wisconsin, not Minnesota, rules, so it is worth working with a lender and title company that regularly handles cross-border St. Croix Valley transactions rather than a purely Minnesota-based team.

How far is Troy Condo Garages from Lake Elmo, MN?

Can a Minnesota resident buy a garage condo in Wisconsin?
