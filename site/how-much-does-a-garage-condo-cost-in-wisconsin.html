What Determines the Price of a Garage Condo in Wisconsin?

Understanding what drives price helps you evaluate any development on its merits — not just its marketing. Here are the five factors that determine what a garage condo in Wisconsin will cost you.

1. Unit Size and Square Footage

Garage condo pricing is driven first by square footage. Larger units and multi-unit or pull-through configurations cost more, and the level of finish and the location within the development also move the price. See available units for current sizes.

2. Included Standard Amenities

Not all garage condos are built the same. The difference between an entry-level unit and a premium one is often in what comes standard. At Troy Condo Garages, the following are included in every unit at no additional cost:

  • In-floor radiant heating (a significant cost at other developments)
  • 150-amp electrical breaker box with service panel
  • 12' × 14' insulated overhead door with automatic opener
  • Fiber optic high-speed internet connection
  • Finished half-bath with shower rough-in
  • Interior water spigot and service door
  • Energy-efficient LED lighting throughout
  • Steel panel siding and concrete slab flooring

Each of these line items carries a cost when installed as an upgrade elsewhere. Buying them as standard inclusions is where the real value calculation favors Hudson.

3. HOA Fees and What They Cover

Monthly HOA fees are a real cost of ownership that buyers sometimes underestimate. At Troy Condo Garages, the HOA fee covers insurance on your unit from the studs out, year-round ground maintenance, snow removal, road upkeep, and common area security. That is materially different from developments where basic maintenance is excluded from the HOA structure and billed separately.

For a full breakdown of what to expect in HOA fee structures at Wisconsin garage condo developments, read our dedicated post on HOA fees at Troy Condo Garages.

4. Property Tax Rate in St. Croix County

Garage condos are deeded real property, which means they carry annual property taxes. Your annual bill depends on the unit's assessed value and the local mill rate. For a complete analysis, read our guide on garage condo property taxes in St. Croix County.

5. Customization and Upgrade Costs

The base unit price is the starting point. Upgrades are optional — depending on what you add, budget anywhere from $0 to $40,000+. Common add-ons include:

  • Epoxy floor coating
  • Two-post or four-post car lift
  • Mezzanine build-out (lounge / office)
  • Mini-split climate control
  • 220V breaker upgrade for heavy tools
  • Custom cabinetry and storage systems

How to Finance a Garage Condo in Wisconsin: Loan Types and What to Expect

Because garage condos are deeded real property — not vehicles or equipment — they are financed differently from what most buyers expect. You are not taking out a personal loan or an auto loan. You are financing real estate.

The most common financing paths for Wisconsin garage condos include:

Commercial Real Estate Loans

This is the most straightforward route. Regional banks and credit unions in western Wisconsin and the Twin Cities area offer commercial real estate loans for garage condo purchases. Down payment, term and rate vary by lender and are based on your credit profile and the appraised value of the unit. Our financing partner can quote current terms.

SBA Loans (for Business Use)

Buyers purchasing a garage condo for business storage or operations may qualify for SBA 504 loans, which offer favorable terms and lower down payment requirements for owner-occupied commercial real estate. This is particularly relevant for contractors, tradespeople, and small business owners in the Hudson area.

Home Equity Financing

Buyers with significant equity in their primary residence sometimes leverage a home equity line of credit (HELOC) or cash-out refinance to fund a garage condo purchase outright or as a large down payment. This simplifies the transaction and eliminates commercial loan underwriting.

Is a Garage Condo in Wisconsin Worth the Price? The Investment Case

Price matters, but so does value. A garage condo is not just a storage solution — it is a real estate asset that can appreciate, generate passive income through leasing, and provide practical value that compounds over time compared to renting storage space.

Consider the math: 10'×20' to 10'×30' storage units in and around Hudson currently list for about $75–$255 per month (SpareFoot listings, October 2026). Over ten years, that is roughly $9,000–$30,000 spent with nothing to show for it. A garage condo builds equity, can be resold, and carries the same practical utility — permanently.
