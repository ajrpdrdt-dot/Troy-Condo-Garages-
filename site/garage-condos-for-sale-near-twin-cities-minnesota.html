What Is a Garage Condo and Why Are Twin Cities Buyers Buying Them?

A garage condo — also called a car condo, motor condo, or vehicle storage condo — is a privately owned, deeded real estate unit purpose-built for vehicle storage, workshops, hobby spaces, or secured equipment storage. Unlike a traditional self-storage unit, which you rent from a company month to month, a garage condo is property you own outright. You hold the deed. You build equity. You can sell, lease, or renovate it as you see fit.

The Twin Cities metro has one of the strongest garage condo markets in the country. According to market data from Only Garage Condos, the suburban Twin Cities saw more than 230 garage condo sales in 2024 alone, with transaction prices spanning from $100,000 to $1.25 million. The hunger for these spaces is real — but so is the competition.

Buyers from Woodbury, Stillwater, Oakdale, Eagan, Apple Valley, and Burnsville routinely search for car condos near St. Paul or garage storage near Minneapolis — only to find that desirable units in the metro are either gone or far outside their budget. That is where western Wisconsin changes the equation.
