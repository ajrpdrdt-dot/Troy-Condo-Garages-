Enclosed Trailer & Cargo Trailer Storage in Hudson, WI: Keep Your Rig Secure Year-Round

Owner's Guide · Hudson, WI · Troy Condo Garages

If you own a cargo trailer, enclosed car trailer, or utility trailer, you already know the problem: most driveways aren't built for them, and leaving one parked outside all year does real damage. Enclosed trailer storage in Hudson, WI at a private garage condo solves the two things outdoor parking can't — security and climate protection — without the compromises of a standard self-storage lot.

Why Outdoor Trailer Storage Costs You More Than You Think

Trailers left outside face problems that are easy to ignore until they're expensive:

  • UV exposure fades and cracks exterior panels and seals over time
  • Freeze-thaw cycles stress axles, tires, and undercarriage components
  • Unsecured lots are a common target for trailer and cargo theft
  • HOA restrictions in many residential neighborhoods prohibit driveway trailer parking altogether

A climate-ready garage condo shell addresses all four at once — something we cover in more depth in our climate control guide.

Winterizing a Trailer Inside a Garage Condo

In-floor heating and insulated, HVAC-ready walls mean your trailer — and anything sensitive stored inside it — is protected from Wisconsin's freeze-thaw cycles. That matters if your cargo trailer carries tools, electronics, or anything with seals and gaskets that degrade in extreme cold. It also means you're not chipping ice off a trailer door every time you need to load up before a trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I fit a 24-foot enclosed trailer in a standard unit?

Yes. Standard units run 20′–25′ x 50′, which comfortably fits most enclosed cargo trailers up to roughly 24 feet with room to walk around it.

Do you have pull-through units for longer trailers?

Yes. Pull-through options start at 25′ x 100′ and up, ideal for car haulers, race trailers, or a trailer paired with a full-size tow vehicle.

Is the facility secure enough for a trailer full of tools or equipment?

Yes. The property has 24-hour video surveillance, gated entry, and a security fence, and each unit has its own locked overhead door under your control.

Can I store my tow vehicle in the same unit as my trailer?

Yes, many owners store the trailer and tow vehicle together, or configure a larger multi-unit space if additional room is needed.

Does in-floor heating protect items stored inside the trailer itself?

Keeping the trailer in a heated, insulated space significantly reduces freeze-thaw stress on its structure and contents compared to leaving it outside, though items sensitive to temperature should still be checked periodically.
