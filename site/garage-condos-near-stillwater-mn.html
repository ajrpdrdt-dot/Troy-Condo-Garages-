Garage Condos Near Stillwater, MN: The Cross-Border Storage Option Twin Cities Collectors Are Missing

Local Buying Guide · Stillwater, MN & Hudson, WI · Troy Condo Garages

If you live in Stillwater, MN and you've searched for a garage condo near Stillwater, you've probably noticed the same thing we hear from buyers every week: Minnesota's east metro is short on private, ownable garage space. What most Stillwater residents don't realize is that a 15-minute drive across the St. Croix River puts them at Troy Condo Garages in Hudson, WI — a facility built specifically for collectors, boaters, and hobbyists who are tired of renting.

Stillwater sits closer to Hudson than it does to most Minneapolis-side storage facilities. For a lot of local car collectors, boat owners, and RV families, that makes Hudson the obvious answer, not a compromise.

Why Stillwater Buyers Look Across the River

Stillwater's own storage inventory skews toward two options: standard self-storage units (chain-link, shared roll-up doors, no ownership) or nothing at all if your lot doesn't allow an oversized detached garage. Neither solves the real problem, which is that serious collectors don't want to rent forever. They want a space they can finish, insure, and eventually sell as real property. That's exactly what a garage condo is designed to be.

  • Stillwater to Troy Condo Garages in Hudson, WI is roughly a 12–15 minute drive via I-94.
  • Wisconsin property tax and HOA structures are often more predictable for a standalone storage asset than trying to shoehorn a build onto a residential MN lot.
  • Hudson's facility was purpose-built for vehicles, boats, and hobby space — not adapted from a warehouse.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Hudson, WI from Stillwater, MN?

It's roughly 12–15 minutes by car via I-94, depending on which part of Stillwater you're starting from.

Can a Minnesota resident buy a garage condo in Wisconsin?

Yes. There is no residency requirement to purchase. Many current owners live in Minnesota and use their unit for storage, hobby space, or workshop use only — residential occupancy is not permitted.

Is buying a garage condo cheaper than renting long-term storage in Stillwater?

Over time, most buyers find ownership offsets or beats rental costs because you build equity instead of paying rent indefinitely. Rental rates in the Twin Cities also tend to rise year over year with no ownership benefit.

What can I store in a garage condo?

Cars, RVs, boats, motorcycles, snowmobiles, trailers, and hobby or workshop equipment are all common. Units are for personal use, storage, and professional hobby space — not for residential living.

Are there units big enough for a boat and trailer combo?

Yes. Standard units run 20′–25′ x 50′, with pull-through options starting at 25′ x 100′ and larger multi-unit configurations available for bigger rigs.
