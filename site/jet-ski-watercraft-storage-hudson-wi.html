Owner's Guide · Hudson, WI

Troy Condo Garages: Published July 8, 2026

Jet ski watercraft storage hudson wi

The St. Croix River corridor near Hudson, WI is one of the busiest recreational boating stretches in the upper Midwest, and jet skis are everywhere on it every summer. What most owners do not think through until fall is where that PWC goes for the other seven months of the year. Marina slips and seasonal haul-out yards work, but they come with recurring fees, waitlists, and zero protection from a Wisconsin winter. A garage condo solves all three, and it does something a rented slip never will: it puts an asset in your name instead of a landlord's.

If you've owned a jet ski, wave runner, or small pontoon for more than one season in western Wisconsin, you already know the seasonal shuffle. Spring means digging the trailer out from behind the garage, hauling the cover off, and hoping the battery still holds a charge. Fall means finding somewhere — anywhere — to stash a boat, trailer, and a bin of life jackets before the first hard freeze. A garage condo at Troy Condo Garages turns that scramble into a five-minute drive to one door you own.

The True Cost of Marina & Seasonal Storage

Marina slip fees for a jet ski or small watercraft in the St. Croix Valley commonly run in the range of $50–$150 per month during the season, plus a separate winter haul-out and storage fee once the water gets too cold to run. Nationally, marina and boat storage pricing follows a similar pattern — Boatsetter's guide to marina fees notes that heated indoor winter storage can cost as much as the seasonal slip itself, on top of whatever you already paid to dock the watercraft all summer. Add trailer storage on top of that if you don't have room at home, plus a haul-out fee, a shrink-wrap or cover fee, and the occasional late-payment penalty if your marina is strict about due dates, and the annual bill adds up quickly — for a spot you never own and can lose access to the moment you miss a renewal.

None of that accounts for waitlists, either. Popular marinas along the St. Croix and the Twin Cities metro routinely fill their slips a full season in advance, which means owners who wait too long end up parking a trailer in the driveway all winter, tarped and exposed to plow trucks, road salt, and freeze-thaw cycling — exactly the conditions that cause the most expensive off-season damage to a PWC.

Why a Garage Condo Works So Well for Watercraft

  • Wide overhead doors make pulling a trailered jet ski or small pontoon in and out simple, even with a full-size tow vehicle
  • In-floor heating and insulated walls protect fuel lines, seals, and upholstery from the freeze-thaw cycling that damages equipment left outside
  • Your own electrical panel lets you run a battery tender through the off-season without asking permission
  • Water spigot access makes rinsing off river silt and cleaning your craft before storage far easier than at a public launch

Local advantage: Hudson, WI sits minutes from the St. Croix River and less than five minutes off I-94, making a garage condo here a genuinely convenient midpoint between your home, the launch, and long-term storage — unlike marinas further up or down river that require a longer haul each trip.

Winterizing Your Jet Ski Before It Goes Into Storage

Where you store a jet ski matters, but how you prep it before storage matters just as much. Freeze damage to the engine and exhaust system is the single most common — and most expensive — mistake owners make heading into a Wisconsin winter. The core steps, outlined in detail by Extra Space Storage's jet ski winterization guide, include flushing the cooling system and exhaust with fresh water and marine antifreeze, adding fuel stabilizer to a topped-off tank, fogging the engine cylinders, disconnecting the battery (or hooking it to a tender), and giving the hull a thorough wash and wax before it goes under cover.

A garage condo makes every one of those steps easier. Instead of racing to winterize in a driveway before the first snowfall, or paying a marina's service department to do it on their schedule, you can work on your own watercraft on your own timeline, indoors, with your own electrical outlet running a battery tender all winter long. If you ever need to redo a step — say, after a warm-weather test run in January — you're not waiting for a haul-out slot to get back to it.

Does a Garage Condo Make Sense as an Investment?

A marina slip is a recurring bill. A garage condo is real property. That distinction matters more than it sounds like it should: every dollar paid toward a slip fee disappears at the end of the season, while a garage condo unit is an asset that can appreciate, be financed, and be sold or passed down like any other piece of real estate. For owners who store a jet ski, a trailer, a tow vehicle, and seasonal gear in the same unit, the math often works out to a comparable or lower annual cost than stacking marina, haul-out, and off-site trailer storage fees separately — with the added benefit of building equity instead of renting space.

Registering Your Watercraft & Staying Legal in Wisconsin

Before you're back on the St. Croix each spring, it's worth double-checking that your PWC's registration, decals, and required safety equipment are current. Wisconsin requires a Certificate of Number and registration decals for all motorized watercraft, including personal watercraft, and registrations run on a three-year cycle through the Wisconsin DNR's boat registration portal. Keeping that paperwork, along with your boating safety certificate, in a dry, organized spot inside your garage condo — rather than buried in a glovebox all winter — is a small habit that saves a scramble every Memorial Day weekend.

Storing More Than Just the PWC

Most owners who bring a jet ski or watercraft into a garage condo end up using the same bay for the tow vehicle, trailer, life jackets, and seasonal gear — consolidating what used to be spread across a marina fee, a driveway, and a shed into one secure, owned space. That consolidation is often the real appeal: one heated, insulated, lockable space replaces a patchwork of a marina contract, a spot in the yard, and whatever corner of the garage at home wasn't already full.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I fit a jet ski trailer and my tow vehicle in one garage condo unit?

Yes, standard 20 to 25 foot by 50 foot units comfortably fit a trailered PWC alongside a tow vehicle, and larger pull-through units can handle multiple watercraft plus gear.

Do I need to winterize my jet ski differently if it's stored in a garage condo?

The winterization process is the same, but a climate-capable, insulated unit reduces the stress of freeze-thaw cycling on fuel lines, seals, and the hull compared to outdoor storage.

Is there water access at Troy Condo Garages for rinsing off equipment?

Yes, units come with an interior water spigot, making it easy to rinse off river silt and clean watercraft before long-term storage.

How far is Troy Condo Garages from the St. Croix River?

The facility is located at 663 Valerie Ave in Hudson, WI, just minutes from the river and less than five minutes off I-94, making it a convenient storage point for river-based recreation.

How much does it cost to store a jet ski for the winter?

Marina and seasonal lot winter storage for a jet ski commonly runs $50 to $150 or more per month once haul-out and heated storage fees are added, and those costs recur every single year with nothing to show for it afterward. A garage condo replaces that recurring fee with ownership of the space itself.

Can I store my jet ski in a regular home garage instead?

You can, but a home garage often has to be shared with a car, a lawn mower, and household storage, and it rarely has the ceiling height, floor drain, or electrical capacity to comfortably run a battery tender and winterize a trailered PWC at the same time. A dedicated garage condo unit is sized specifically for recreational vehicles and watercraft.

Do I need to winterize my jet ski every year, even if I stored it indoors?

Yes. Indoor, climate-protected storage reduces the stress of freeze-thaw cycling, but it doesn't replace the need to flush the cooling system, stabilize the fuel, and disconnect or tend the battery each off-season.

What size storage unit do I need for a jet ski and trailer?

Most trailered personal watercraft fit comfortably in a 20 to 25 foot wide bay, and Troy Condo Garages' larger pull-through units can accommodate a jet ski trailer, tow vehicle, and additional gear in the same space.

Is owning a garage condo a good investment compared to renting storage?

Because a garage condo is titled real property rather than a recurring rental fee, owners build equity over time instead of paying into a marina or storage facility indefinitely, which is a meaningful difference for anyone who plans to keep their watercraft for several seasons.
