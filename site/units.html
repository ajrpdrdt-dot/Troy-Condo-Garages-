Frequently asked questions

What is a garage condo?

A privately owned unit that provides secure storage and personal-use space — workshop, vehicle storage, and more.

Are these units real property?

Yes. Units available for purchase are recorded as real property. Rental inventory, when offered, is separate.

Can I store an RV or boat?

Yes. Oversized units are designed for RVs, boats, and multiple vehicles.

Where are you located?

663 Valerie Ave, Hudson, WI — convenient for Hudson and the Twin Cities area.
